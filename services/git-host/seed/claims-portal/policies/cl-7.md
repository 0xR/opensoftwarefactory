# CL-7: Why a Claim Was Rejected

Owner: compliance officer. Version: 3.

Only a claims handler tells a customer
why a claim was rejected, by phone or
by letter. The portal and the app show
"Rejected" and the phone number of
the claims handler.

## Rule for the claim status panel

For a rejected claim, the panel text is exactly
"Rejected. Call your claims handler:" and the phone number.
Any other text fails.

Must pass: "Rejected. Call your claims handler: 020 123 4567."
Must fail: "Rejected: the damage happened before your policy started."

CL-7 allows no exceptions.
